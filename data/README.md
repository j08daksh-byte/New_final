# Data

`train_FD001.txt`, `test_FD001.txt` and `RUL_FD001.txt` are the FD001 subset of the **C-MAPSS Turbofan
Engine Degradation Simulation** data set from the NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence.

A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon, N. Eklund, "Damage Propagation Modeling for Aircraft Engine
Run-to-Failure Simulation", International Conference on Prognostics and Health Management (PHM08), 2008.

The files are unmodified. Each row is one operating cycle of one engine: unit number, cycle, three
operating settings and 21 sensor readings. The data are produced by NASA's engine simulator, not
recorded from engines in service.
